💬 What are these cards for?

These cards practise the building blocks of chemistry: element symbols, key words, counting atoms, common molecules, molecular models and phases of matter. You need all of these to do naming compounds and balancing equations, which are coming next.

The cards are shuffled, so you never know what is next! Use the menu to practise one category if it feels hard. Mistakes are good — they show us what to practise.

In this deck: element symbols · key words · physical & chemical change · counting atoms · common molecules · molecular models · phases of matter.

NOT in this deck yet — you still need these for tests and the exam. We will practise them later:

  • Naming compounds & writing their formulas (ionic, molecular, polyatomic ions)
  • Balancing chemical equations
  • Atoms in detail: protons, neutrons, electrons, Bohr-Rutherford diagrams, valence electrons, ions
  • Periodic table patterns (groups, periods, metals & non-metals)
  • Ionic vs molecular compounds
  • Types of reactions: synthesis, decomposition, single & double displacement, combustion
  • Acids & bases: the pH scale, indicators, neutralization
  • What makes reactions faster (collision theory)
  • Chemistry, safety & the environment (e.g., acid rain, WHMIS safety symbols)
