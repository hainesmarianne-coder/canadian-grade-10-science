💬 What are these cards for?

These cards practise how your body (and a plant!) is organized: cells make tissues, tissues make organs, and organs work together in systems. You need this for the Unit 1 test and the exam.

The cards are shuffled, so you never know what is next! Use the menu to practise one category if it feels hard. Mistakes are good — they show us what to practise.

✍️ Command-word cards. In tests, the first word of a question usually tells you what kind of answer to write. On these cards, ALL the answers are true science — but only ONE answers the command word. After you choose, each answer shows which command word it really answers.
